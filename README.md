# project-2-data-analysis
A quick data dive into what actually affects student energy and focus. Surveyed 70 students about exercise, phone use, sleep, and study habits—then analyzed it all with Python(pandas, matplotlib, seaborn).  Showed that less physical activity resulted in less concentration.


Student Energy and Focus Analysis

Overview

This project examines the relationship between daily student habits including physical activity, phone usage, sleep, and study time and self-reported energy and concentration levels. Survey data from 70 students was analyzed using descriptive statistics and data visualization to identify patterns and test hypotheses about student productivity.

Research Question

How do physical activity, phone usage, sleep, and study habits affect student energy and concentration?

Dataset

The data was collected via a Google Forms survey. The sample consists of 70 university and school students.

Key variables include:

Exercise measured in days per week
Energy level on a scale of 1 to 10
Focus level on a scale of 1 to 10
Sleep measured in hours per night
Phone usage measured in hours per day
Study time measured in hours per day

Tools Used

Python 3
Pandas for data manipulation
Matplotlib and Seaborn for visualization
Jupyter Notebook for analysis

Files

final_analyzed_data.csv contains the cleaned dataset from the survey
final_code.ipynb is the Jupyter notebook containing the full analysis
analytica_report.pdf is the complete research report
energy_distribution.png is a histogram of student energy levels
phone_focus_trend.png is a bar chart comparing phone usage and average focus
study_proportion.png is a pie chart showing study time distribution

Key Findings

The mean focus score was 5.56 out of 10
The mean energy score was 5.79 out of 10
Students who reported 4 to 6 hours of daily phone use had focus scores at or below the average
Most students exercise 1 to 2 days per week and study 1 to 2 hours per day
Higher phone usage was associated with lower concentration levels
